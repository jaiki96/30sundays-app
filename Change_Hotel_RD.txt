CHANGE HOTEL SCREEN - REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT
Screen: Change hotel (hotel alternatives for one stay)
Reference: the Change day plan screen, which this now matches
Prototype: live on the 30 Sundays prototype

------------------------------------------------------------
1. BACKGROUND
------------------------------------------------------------
The Change hotel screen was a comparison table pretending to be a list.
Each hotel was a small thumbnail with six lines of dense text beside it,
and the hotel the customer already had was pinned to the top of the list
inside a box of its own.

Two problems with that:

  - A hotel is looked at before it is read. The photo was the smallest
    thing on the card.
  - Choosing a stay is the same kind of decision as choosing a day plan.
    We had already solved that on the Change day plan screen, and the two
    screens behaved nothing like each other.

So Change hotel now uses the Change day plan pattern: photo led cards, a
short set of facts under them, sorting and filters in one tabbed sheet,
and a floating bar of quick filters.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. SCOPE
------------------------------------------------------------
In scope:

  - The hotel alternatives list for one stay of the itinerary.
  - Its header, search, sorting, filters and photo gallery.
  - The photographs used on hotel cards across the app.

Not in scope:

  - The hotel detail screen, which is unchanged. It continues to carry
    the room choice, the "Replacing X" strip and the Replace hotel CTA.
  - The cost breakdown and the save flow on the itinerary.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. HEADER
------------------------------------------------------------
The header is one strip, never more than two lines tall.

  - Line 1: the place name only, for example "Ubud". Not "Stay in Ubud",
    not the hotel name. Beside it, in a lighter weight, the number of
    options found, for example "9 options".
  - Line 2: the stay dates, the number of rooms, and the number of
    travellers shown as a person icon plus a figure. Example:
    "Jun 20 - Jun 22 · 1 room · [icon] 2".
  - The day range (Day 1 to 3) and the nights count are not shown. The
    dates already say both.
  - Back arrow on the left.
  - A search icon on the right. Tapping it replaces lines 1 and 2 with a
    full width search field, and tapping it again clears and closes it.
    The strip height does not change when search opens.
  - An overflow menu on the far right holds the secondary action,
    "Book this stay myself".

Search rules:

  - Matches on hotel name and on area or landmark name.
  - Case insensitive, matches anywhere in the name, not only the start.
  - The option count in the header updates with the search.
  - Search and filters apply together, not one instead of the other.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE CURRENT HOTEL
------------------------------------------------------------
  - The hotel already in the itinerary is NOT shown in the list. It is
    not an alternative to itself.
  - It is used as the price baseline. Every card shows the difference
    from it, never an absolute price.
  - The customer is reminded what they are replacing on the hotel detail
    screen, which already says "Replacing {hotel name}" with the old
    price, the new price and the difference.
  - If for any reason there is no current hotel on the stay (a stay with
    no hotel selected yet), the cards show the total price for the stay
    instead of a difference, and nothing else changes.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE HOTEL CARD
------------------------------------------------------------
Order of elements, top to bottom:

5.1 Photos

  - A horizontally swipeable strip at the top of the card, each photo
    slightly less than full card width so the next one peeks in and
    invites the swipe.
  - A badge in the bottom right corner showing the total photo count,
    for example "14 photos".
  - Tapping any photo, or the badge, opens the full screen gallery. It
    must not open the hotel.
  - Tapping anywhere else on the card opens the hotel detail screen.

5.2 Hotel name

  - Full name, up to two lines.

5.3 Two quality chips, side by side

  - Star category: a filled star icon and "5 star". This is our own
    categorisation.
  - Booking.com score: the Booking.com mark, the score, and the word
    "Rated", for example "9.6 Rated". The number of people who rated is
    NOT shown on the card.
  - Both chips are white with a hairline border, and the colour is
    carried by the border and the text, the same treatment the day
    rating and day pace chips use on Change day plan.
  - The score chip is colour coded by the score itself:
      9.0 and above: green
      8.0 to 8.9: teal
      below 8.0: amber
  - The star chip is amber.

5.4 Room and location, one plain line

  - Format: "{room name} · {area}, {distance} km from city centre".
  - Example: "Executive Suite · Tegallalang, 2.4 km from city centre".
  - The words "city centre" must be used, not "centre" on its own.
  - The room shown is the cheapest room of that hotel, which is also the
    room the price difference is based on.

5.5 Inclusions row, an icon and a word each

  - Each fact is an icon plus a short label, spaced apart. They are not
    joined by dots and they are not chips, so the row stays light and
    does not read as a continuation of the line above it.
  - The facts, in this order:
      Meal plan, when the room includes one. Show the plan as it is,
      for example "Breakfast included" or "Breakfast + Dinner". Hide
      this fact entirely when the room is room only.
      Transfer: "Shared transfer" or "Private transfer".
      Refundability: "Refundable" or "Non-refundable". Non-refundable
      is greyed, so the absence reads as an absence.
      "Early check-in", only when the hotel offers it.
      "Late checkout", only when the hotel offers it.
  - The row wraps to a second line when there are more facts than fit.
    Wrapping is expected and acceptable.

5.6 Price difference

  - The only money on the card. Never the absolute price.
  - Shown as a filled chip, the same shape Change day plan uses:
      "More by ₹4,800" in red on a light red fill.
      "Lesser by ₹600" in green on a light green fill.
      "No change in price" in plain grey text when the difference is nil.
  - The figure is the difference for the WHOLE stay, not per night.
  - The number of nights is not repeated on the card. The header already
    carries the dates.
  - A chevron sits at the far right of the same row to say the card
    opens.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. PHOTO GALLERY
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Opens full screen over the list, on a dark background.
  - Opens on the photo that was tapped, not always on the first one.
  - Header shows the hotel name and a counter, "5 of 14".
  - Swipes horizontally, one photo per screen, and the counter follows.
  - Closes with the X, with the device back gesture, or with Escape on
    a keyboard. Closing returns to the same scroll position in the list.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. PHOTOGRAPHS
------------------------------------------------------------
This applies wherever hotel photos appear, not only on this screen.

  - Hotel photos must show the hotel. Destination scenery is not a hotel
    photo and must not stand in for one.
  - Every hotel carries at least eight photos, grouped by what they show:
    room, bathroom, pool, exterior and grounds, restaurant.
  - The order they are swiped in is fixed, so every hotel tells the same
    story: room, pool, room, exterior, bathroom, restaurant, room, pool.
  - The first photo on a card is always a room.
  - Each room type carries its own photos: two of the room, one of its
    bathroom, one of the view or the grounds.
  - When a hotel has fewer photos than the pattern needs, repeat nothing.
    Show what exists and let the count be honest.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. SORTING
------------------------------------------------------------
Sorting sits in the Sort tab of the same sheet as the filters.

  - Recommended (default). Best rated and highest category first.
  - Price difference: low to high.
  - Price difference: high to low.
  - Rating: high to low, on the Booking.com score.
  - Distance: nearest first, from the city centre.

Notes:

  - Sorting is by price DIFFERENCE, not by price, because every hotel on
    this screen is a change to an existing stay.
  - Choosing a sort closes the sheet immediately. There is no separate
    apply step for sorting.
  - The chosen sort survives filter changes and is not reset by
    "Reset all".

------------------------------------------------------------
9. FILTERS
------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 Quick filters, on the floating bar

  - One tap each, scrollable horizontally: 5 star, 9+ rated, Free
    cancellation, Private transfer, Early check-in.
  - They write the same filter state the sheet does, so the two can
    never disagree.

9.2 Filter sheet

  - Hotel category: 3 star, 4 star, 5 star. Multiple allowed.
  - Booking.com rating: 8+, 8.5+, 9+. One at a time.
  - Meals: Only Room, Breakfast included, Breakfast + Dinner, All
    Inclusive. Multiple allowed. A hotel matches if any of its rooms
    offers a selected plan.
  - Nearby landmarks: a searchable list of the areas in that city, five
    visible with the rest behind "Show more".
  - Hotel facilities: searchable list. A hotel must offer all selected
    facilities.
  - Room facilities: searchable list. Some room of the hotel must offer
    each selected facility.

9.3 Removed

  - Budget. The card shows a difference, not a price, so a price range
    filter contradicts the screen.

9.4 Sheet behaviour

  - Two tabs, Filters and Sort, in one sheet.
  - A count badge on the filter button shows how many filters are in
    effect, quick filters included.
  - The sheet footer is pinned: "Reset all" on the left, and a primary
    button on the right reading "Show N hotels", where N updates live as
    filters change.
  - Filters apply as they are tapped. The primary button closes the
    sheet, it does not apply anything that was not already applied.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. SCENARIOS AND STATES
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Stay with a hotel already chosen: list excludes it, every card shows
    a difference from it.
  - Stay with no hotel chosen: every card shows the stay total instead of
    a difference. Sorting by price difference falls back to sorting by
    that total.
  - Only one alternative available: the list shows one card, the header
    reads "1 option", and the sheet button reads "Show 1 hotel".
  - No alternatives at all: show the empty state, not an empty list.
  - Filters or search match nothing: show "Nothing matches those
    filters", a line suggesting a filter be cleared or a hotel searched
    for, and a "Clear filters" button that also clears the search.
  - Long room names: the room and location line wraps to two lines. It
    must not be truncated, because the room name is what the price is
    based on.
  - Long hotel names: wrap to two lines, then truncate.
  - Prices unavailable for a hotel: do not show a card with a blank
    price. Leave the hotel out of the list.
  - Self booking: the overflow menu offers "Book this stay myself". It
    asks for confirmation, explains that the hotel and its cost leave
    the package, and returns the customer to the itinerary. This is
    unchanged behaviour and must be kept.

------------------------------------------------------------
11. NAVIGATION
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Back returns the customer to wherever they came from, either the
    itinerary or the hotel detail screen. It must not always force them
    to the itinerary.
  - Tapping a card opens the hotel detail screen, carrying the current
    hotel, the deal and the version along in the link, so the detail
    screen can still show "Replacing {hotel name}" and its price
    difference.
  - The gallery, the filter sheet and the screen itself close in that
    order on back or Escape. Back never skips two levels at once.

------------------------------------------------------------
12. EVENTS TO BE TRACKED
------------------------------------------------------------
Every event carries customer_id, deal_id, version_id, itinerary_id,
stay_index, city and current_hotel_id where they apply.

12.1 Screen and list

  - change_hotel_opened
    Properties: option_count, current_hotel_id, current_hotel_price,
    nights, entry_point (itinerary or hotel_detail).

  - change_hotel_card_viewed
    Fires when a card enters the viewport.
    Properties: hotel_id, position, star_category, booking_score,
    price_delta, refundable, transfer_type.

  - change_hotel_search_opened

  - change_hotel_searched
    Properties: query, result_count.

12.2 Photos

  - change_hotel_photos_swiped
    Properties: hotel_id, photos_seen.

  - change_hotel_gallery_opened
    Properties: hotel_id, opened_from (photo or badge), photo_count.

  - change_hotel_gallery_photo_viewed
    Properties: hotel_id, photo_index, category.

  - change_hotel_gallery_closed
    Properties: hotel_id, photos_seen, duration_ms.

12.3 Sorting and filters

  - change_hotel_sort_opened

  - change_hotel_sorted
    Properties: sort_key, result_count.

  - change_hotel_quick_filter_toggled
    Properties: filter_key, on (true or false), result_count.

  - change_hotel_filters_opened
    Properties: active_filter_count.

  - change_hotel_filter_applied
    Properties: filter_group, filter_value, on, result_count.

  - change_hotel_filters_reset
    Properties: cleared_count.

  - change_hotel_no_results
    Properties: active_filters, query.

12.4 Outcomes

  - change_hotel_card_tapped
    Properties: hotel_id, position, price_delta, sort_key,
    active_filter_count, from_search (true or false).

  - change_hotel_replaced
    Fires when the customer confirms the change on the detail screen.
    Properties: from_hotel_id, to_hotel_id, price_delta, position,
    sort_key, active_filter_count, photos_seen.

  - change_hotel_abandoned
    Fires when the customer leaves without changing the hotel.
    Properties: cards_viewed, galleries_opened, duration_ms.

  - change_hotel_self_book_selected
    Properties: city, removed_cost.

------------------------------------------------------------
13. WHAT WE WANT TO LEARN FROM THE DATA
------------------------------------------------------------
  - How many customers who open this screen actually change the hotel,
    and how that compares with the old screen.
  - Whether photo-led cards raise the change rate, measured through
    photos_seen and galleries_opened against change_hotel_replaced.
  - Which sort customers choose, and whether price difference low to
    high is the one that leads to a change.
  - Which filters are used, and whether removing budget cost us
    anything.
  - The position in the list of the hotel finally chosen, which tells us
    whether our Recommended order is any good.
  - How often customers move to a dearer hotel, and by how much, which
    is the revenue case for this screen.

------------------------------------------------------------
14. OPEN POINTS FOR SIGN OFF
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Confirm the score bands for the colour coding of the Booking.com
    chip (currently 9+ green, 8 to 8.9 teal, under 8 amber).
  - Confirm whether hotel facilities and room facilities should stay in
    the filter sheet. They were kept because only budget was asked to be
    removed.
  - Confirm the minimum number of photos a hotel must have before it can
    be listed, and who is responsible for supplying them.
  - Confirm whether the price difference should be the difference for
    the cheapest room, as it is today, or for a room of the same type as
    the current booking.
  - Confirm whether hotels with no availability for the dates should be
    hidden or shown as unavailable.

ITINERARY AT A GLANCE - DAY-WISE VIDEOS (HALF PAGER)
Screen: Itinerary detail  |  For UI team review

------------------------------------------------------------
BACKGROUND
------------------------------------------------------------
Today the itinerary detail screen has a dedicated section called
"Watch your trip in videos" (highlights, day-wise videos and a day
score). Data and screen-recording analysis show not many users
reach or open this section.

We want to move "Itinerary at a glance" to the top so the day plan
is the first thing users see. But on its own the first scroll then
reads as a wall of text.

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WE WANT
------------------------------------------------------------
Bring the videos to where users already look. Show the day-wise
videos inline, against each day card, inside "Itinerary at a glance".

  - "Itinerary at a glance" becomes the first section on the screen.
  - Each day card shows that day's video(s) right on the card, so the
    first scroll has visuals, not just text.
  - Tapping a video opens the player as it does today.
  - The existing "Change day plan" action stays on each day card.

------------------------------------------------------------
OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE UI TEAM
------------------------------------------------------------
  - How should a day's videos sit on the card: one cover thumbnail
    with a play button, or a small row of thumbnails if the day has
    several?
  - For a leisure or transfer day with no activity video, what do we
    show (a destination clip, an image, or nothing)?
  - Do we keep the day score, and if so where: on the card, behind a
    tap, or dropped?
  - Do we remove the standalone "Watch your trip in videos" section
    once videos live on the day cards, or keep a highlights-only
    version of it?
  - How do we keep the card compact so the list still scans quickly?

------------------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS SIGNALS
------------------------------------------------------------
  - More users watch day videos (now that they are in the main flow).
  - First scroll feels visual, not text heavy.
  - Users engage with the day plan earlier in the session.

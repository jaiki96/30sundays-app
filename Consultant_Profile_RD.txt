TRAVEL CONSULTANT PROFILE - REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT
For UI team review / second opinion
Screen: Itinerary detail (customer's trip plan)

------------------------------------------------------------
1. BACKGROUND
------------------------------------------------------------
Every trip is handled by a travel consultant. Right now the
customer does not see who is looking after their trip on the
itinerary screen. Showing the consultant's profile builds trust
and gives the customer a clear person to reach out to.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT WE WANT
------------------------------------------------------------
Once a travel consultant is assigned to the trip, show their
profile on the Itinerary detail screen.

Details to show:
  - Profile picture
  - Name
  - Bio (short, 2 lines)
  - An option to call

Rule:
  - Only show this once a consultant is actually assigned. If no
    consultant is assigned yet, show nothing.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. TWO DISPLAY OPTIONS TO DECIDE BETWEEN
------------------------------------------------------------
Option A - Section on the screen
  - A dedicated section within the itinerary, showing the picture,
    name, bio and a call button, all visible at once.
  - Pros: always visible while scrolling to that point, feels part
    of the trip, more reassuring.
  - Cons: takes vertical space and competes with other sections.

Option B - Sticky icon or card
  - A small sticky element (icon or compact card) that stays on
    screen. On tap it opens the full details (picture, name, bio,
    call).
  - Pros: always reachable, takes little space, can call from
    anywhere on the screen.
  - Cons: less discoverable, the profile is one tap away instead of
    in view.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE UI TEAM
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Section or sticky element, which fits this screen better?
  - If sticky, should it be an icon (photo bubble) or a small card
    with the name always visible?
  - Where should it sit so it does not clash with the customisation
    controls and the bottom navigation?
  - Should "call" be a direct call, or open a small sheet with call
    plus other contact options later?
  - Do we want the consultant profile before or after the customer
    has customised the trip?

------------------------------------------------------------
5. SUCCESS SIGNALS
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Customers recognise they have a named person on their trip.
  - More trust and fewer "who is handling my trip" queries.
  - Healthy use of the call option when customers need help.

Hotel list changes

Four changes to Change hotel, the list of hotels for one city. Shown on Ubud. Open the links on a phone or in a narrow window.

Live at30sundays-activities.vercel.app

1. Gold tag to shortlist quickly

A gold tag with a crown on the photo of the two best-reviewed hotels in each city, like Luxe on Agoda. A "Gold" quick filter, first in the bottom bar, shows just those.

See the list

2. SIC tag removed

The shared or private transfer line is gone from every hotel card. Private transfer is gone from the quick filters too. Filters now has a Transfers section with one option: "Shared transfer available".

See the list

3. Hotel area line kept

Each card still shows its area and distance, e.g. "Tegallalang · 2.4 km from city centre".

4. Where to stay guide at the top

A postcard at the top of the list, in the style of the city page's "Hanoi, honestly" card: "Where to stay in Ubud", the advice ("A pool villa, not a hotel room") and Read more. Read more opens a bottom drawer with what kind of hotel and which room to look for.

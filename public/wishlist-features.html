Wishlist

Everything a person saves, filed by destination, and a way to turn it into a trip in two steps. One-person boards ship first; the group board comes later. Open the links on a phone or in a narrow window.

Filters in the map's colours

Part 4

Pins on the map

One pin shape, five colours

A white pin with a coloured disc and a white icon. Colours from the brand shade card: things to do pink FE6795, regions forest 516968, hotels lagoon 00A898, food golden FDB534. Itineraries (forest 254342) have no pin.

See the pins

Names without a chip

The name sits on the map in its pin's colour, with a white glow. It goes right of the pin, then left, and hides only when both sides are taken. Zoom in to see it.

Where each kind is placed

Things to do and regions: their real spot. Food: its neighbourhood. Hotels: their address for the Maldives resorts and Alila Seminyak, otherwise the town's centre. Pins on the same spot sit side by side.

Choosing cities

Saved cities first, under "From your wishlist · 2 saved", then "All cities". Nothing pre-ticked. Every card shows a selection circle.

Changing a hotel

Saved hotels sit on a pale pink shelf at the top, "Your wishlist stays", with "2 of your saves are in Ubud". Swipe it sideways. Then "All hotels in Ubud". Saved hotels get a heart on their map price pin.

Changing a day

A row on top, "Include activities from your wishlist": the saved things to do this day can hold, each a small icon and name, scrolling sideways. Ticking one shows only the plans that include it (Ubud: 12 plans to 4). Plans holding a save still say "You saved X" on the photo.

Part 8

Ways into the wishlist

On home

A white card under "Why we're the perfect match". Chips switch destination, each with its count. Three photos of the latest saves, a count per kind, and "Shortlist more in Bali". The photos open the board; "See all" opens the wishlist.

A deck of regions, stays, things to do and places to eat, mixed, leaving out what is already saved. Swipe right or tap Wishlist to save; swipe left or tap Not for me to skip. A hint shows on first open. No progress count. Not logged in? The first save asks for login, then the deck carries on.

Part 9

How much of the wishlist a trip covers

One line on the itinerary

Under the trip's title: a small ring and "30% of your Bali wishlist is in this trip", with View. Cities, stays and things to do count; food and shops do not, since an itinerary does not book them. Hidden when nothing is saved for that destination.

Swing & Sanur

The sheet behind View

The percentage in a ring, "3 of your 10 Bali saves are in", then Cities, Stays and Things to do. What is in shows its days ("Day 1 - 2"). What is not gets a small grey Add or Swap in.

Add and Swap in

A city opens the cities screen. A stay opens that town's hotel list. A thing to do opens that town's day plans with it already ticked.

Known gaps

  1. Hotel lists are generated per town, so a saved hotel can appear in another town's list with a different score (W Bali Seminyak shows in Ubud at 8.3, saved at 8.4).
  2. Most hotels have no address yet, so their pin sits on the town's centre.
  3. Some places to eat have no map position (Locavore NXT), so they show in the list but not as a pin.
  4. Hotel score: out of 10 (Booking) or out of 5 like everything else? Still open.
  5. Planning from a saved itinerary keeps the dates picked, even when they differ from its length.
  6. Coverage counts a thing to do only when a day plan names it. Swing & Sanur starts at 30% until a day is swapped to one with a saved activity.
  7. Some photos in the deck come from the app's own data and do not match the name (Sacred Monkey Forest shows rice terraces).

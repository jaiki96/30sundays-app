HOME TRIP CARD - REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT
For UI team review / second opinion
Screen: Home

------------------------------------------------------------
1. BACKGROUND
------------------------------------------------------------
The app shows a sticky card at the footer. Its content changes by
user stage: New, Lead, Pre trip, On trip and Post trip.

For Lead, Pre trip and Post trip users, the most important thing is
their itinerary or trip. Today, to reach it, they have to either tap
the sticky footer card, or go to Trips / My plan and then tap a card
to open the Itinerary or Trip detail. That is one or two extra steps
to get to the thing they care about most.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT WE WANT
------------------------------------------------------------
Redesign the Home screen for Lead, Pre trip and Post trip users so
the trip is front and centre.

  - Show a trip card as the hero item at the top of Home.
  - The card carries the crucial trip information at a glance.
  - Tapping the card takes the user straight to the Itinerary or
    Trip detail screen.

Reference: District shows a similar card on the home screen once a
user books a movie ticket, so the booking is the first thing you see.

New users keep the current Home. This change is only for Lead, Pre
trip and Post trip.

Note: the brief mentioned Post trip as both "fine as is" and part of
this group. Assuming Post trip is included. Please confirm.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT THE CARD SHOWS (by stage)
------------------------------------------------------------
The card should show what matters most at each stage. Suggested,
open for the team to refine:

  Lead
    - Destination and trip dates
    - Trip status (for example: itinerary ready / being prepared)
    - Clear prompt to open the itinerary

  Pre trip
    - Destination and travel dates
    - Countdown or days to go
    - Next thing to do (for example: complete payment, add details)

  Post trip
    - Destination and travel dates
    - A wrap-up or memory prompt, or next-trip nudge

------------------------------------------------------------
4. BEHAVIOUR
------------------------------------------------------------
  - The card is the hero item, above everything else on Home.
  - Tap anywhere on the card opens the Itinerary or Trip detail.
  - If the user has more than one active trip, decide how to handle
    it (see open questions).
  - The footer sticky card: decide whether it stays, changes, or is
    removed for these stages to avoid showing the same thing twice.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE UI TEAM
------------------------------------------------------------
  - What is the single most useful piece of info per stage on the
    card?
  - If the sticky footer card and the hero card both point to the
    trip, do we keep both, or drop the footer one for these stages?
  - How do we handle users with multiple trips (carousel, most
    recent only, or a small list)?
  - Should the card look different per stage, or be one consistent
    design with changing content?
  - Confirm whether Post trip is in scope for this change.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. SUCCESS SIGNALS
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Fewer taps to reach the itinerary or trip detail.
  - More users open their trip from Home directly.
  - Lower drop-off between Home and the itinerary for Leads and Pre
    trip users.

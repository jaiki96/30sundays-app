HOME TRIP CARD - 1 PAGER
Screen: Home  |  Users: Lead, Pre trip, Post trip  |  For UI team review

PROBLEM
Today the trip lives behind the footer sticky card, or behind Trips /
My plan. Lead, Pre trip and Post trip users take one or two extra taps
to reach the thing they care about most: their itinerary.

PROPOSAL
Make the trip the hero of Home for these users. Show a trip card at the
top of Home with the crucial trip info. Tapping it opens the Itinerary
or Trip detail directly. (Same idea as District showing your booking on
home after you buy a movie ticket.) New users keep the current Home.

WHAT THE CARD SHOWS (by stage, open to refine)
  Lead      Destination, dates, status, prompt to open itinerary
  Pre trip  Destination, dates, days to go, next action to take
  Post trip Destination, dates, trip wrap-up or next-trip nudge

BEHAVIOUR
  - Hero item, above everything else on Home.
  - Tap anywhere on the card opens Itinerary / Trip detail.
  - Decide if the footer sticky card stays or is dropped for these
    stages to avoid showing the trip twice.
  - Decide how to handle users with more than one active trip.

OPEN QUESTIONS
  - Single most useful info per stage on the card?
  - Keep both cards (footer + hero) or drop the footer for these stages?
  - Multiple trips: carousel, latest only, or short list?
  - One design with changing content, or a different look per stage?
  - Confirm Post trip is in scope (brief mentioned it both ways).

SUCCESS SIGNALS
  - Fewer taps to reach the itinerary / trip detail.
  - More trips opened straight from Home.
  - Lower Home to itinerary drop-off for Leads and Pre trip.

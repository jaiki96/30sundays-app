PAST PLANS IN MY PLANS - REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT
Screens: My Plans, Itinerary detail, Itinerary builder (internal)
Prototype: live on the 30 Sundays prototype

------------------------------------------------------------
1. BACKGROUND
------------------------------------------------------------
Today My Plans shows only deals in the active state. The system
allows only one active deal per customer at a time. Any deal marked
lost disappears from the customer's app completely.

This is a loss on both sides:

  - The customer loses every itinerary we ever built for them. They
    come back a year later and have nothing to restart from.
  - The sales consultant has no way to point the customer at an old
    itinerary, and the customer has no way to point the consultant at
    one either.

We want lost deals to remain visible to the customer, in a collapsed
Past plans area, with a number the customer can read out on a call.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. VOCABULARY USED IN THIS DOCUMENT
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Enquiry: one conversation with the customer. One enquiry can hold
    several countries. Example: a customer enquires once and we build
    Vietnam and Thailand for them.
  - Deal: the existing deal record. Today, one deal per destination
    inside an enquiry.
  - Version: one finalised itinerary (a quote with a PDF) inside a
    deal. A deal can hold many versions.
  - Plan ID: the customer facing number. See section 4.
  - Past plan: an enquiry whose deals are all in the lost state.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHY PAST PLANS ARE NOT GROUPED BY DESTINATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Live plans in My Plans are grouped by destination, and that is
correct, because inside one live enquiry "my Bali plan" is a real
thing the customer recognises.

A past enquiry is not a destination, it is a moment in time.

  - One past enquiry can hold several countries.
  - The same country can appear in the live enquiry and in two
    different past enquiries.
  - If we group past plans by country, Bali shows up three times, and
    versions from different enquiries with different prices sit next
    to each other as though they belong together.

Therefore:

  - Past plans are grouped by ENQUIRY.
  - Enquiries are sorted by TIME, newest first.
  - Inside an enquiry, the countries are listed together on one row.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. PLAN ID
------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: a consultant searching the internal tool by the customer's
mobile number finds several enquiries and a stack of itineraries
against one customer, often more than one for the same country.
"The Bali one" does not narrow it down. The Plan ID does.

Rules:

  - The Plan ID is a plain numeric identifier, 10 to 12 digits.
  - It is derived from the internal deal ID. Deal ID is the source of
    truth. The Plan ID is only the customer facing presentation of
    it. It must resolve back to exactly one record in the internal
    tool.
  - There are two levels:
      Enquiry level Plan ID: identifies the enquiry. Shown once on
      the past enquiry row.
      Itinerary level Plan ID: identifies one finalised itinerary
      (one version). Shown on each itinerary card.
  - The Plan ID does not encode country or version number. The card
    already shows the country and the version, so repeating them in
    the number only makes it longer to read out.
  - The Plan ID never changes once issued, including when the
    customer plans that trip again. The new plan gets its own new ID.
  - The Plan ID must be tappable to copy, with a short confirmation
    after copy.
  - Plan ID is shown only on past plans. Live plan cards must not
    show a Plan ID strip. Live cards stay exactly as they are today.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT IS SHOWN IN MY PLANS
------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Live plans

  - No change at all. Same cards, same grouping, same actions, same
    order. No Plan ID strip on them.

5.2 Past plans section

  - Sits below all live plans, separated by a divider.
  - Collapsed by default, with the header "Past plans (N)" where N is
    the number of past enquiries, not the number of itineraries.
  - Tapping the header expands the list of past enquiries.

5.3 Past enquiry row (collapsed state of one enquiry)

  - Thumbnail images of up to two destinations from that enquiry,
    overlapped, shown desaturated so they read as inactive.
  - Line 1: "Planned in {Month Year}", taken from the most recent
    activity in that enquiry.
  - Line 2: the countries in the enquiry, separated by a middle dot.
    Each country appears only once.
  - Line 3: "{N} itineraries - Plan {enquiry Plan ID}". Use the
    singular "itinerary" when N is 1. N counts finalised itineraries
    (quotes) only, not drafts.
  - A chevron indicating expandable.

5.4 Past enquiry expanded

  - A short note at the top: "Prices are the ones quoted in {Month
    Year} and are no longer valid. Start any of these again and we
    will price it afresh."
  - One card per itinerary inside the enquiry, in the same card
    format as My Plans uses today, but muted and marked as expired.
  - The card must not repeat the country name that is already shown
    on the enquiry row above it.
  - Each card shows its own itinerary level Plan ID, tappable to
    copy.
  - Each card shows a footer note "Quoted {Month Year}" and a single
    action, "Plan this again".

5.5 Empty and mixed states

  - Live plans present and past plans present: as described above.
  - Only past plans, no live plan: the past plans section is shown
    expanded by default so the screen is not empty, and the normal
    "start a new trip" entry point stays visible above it.
  - No live plans and no past plans: the screen stays exactly as the
    current empty state. No past plans section is rendered.

Scope note for the first release: only the live plus past state is
being released. The past only state and the nothing yet state are
designed but held back, to keep the release simple.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHICH LOST DEALS QUALIFY TO BE SHOWN
------------------------------------------------------------
Not every lost deal deserves to be in the customer's app.

  - Show a lost deal only if at least one finalised itinerary (a
    quote with a PDF) actually reached the customer.
  - Do not show lost deals that never got past the draft stage.
  - Do not show duplicate enquiries, test deals, or deals created by
    the team for internal checks.
  - Do not show deals belonging to a cancelled booking. Cancellations
    are a different state and are out of scope for this document.
  - If an enquiry ends up with zero qualifying itineraries after
    these filters, the whole enquiry is hidden.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. PLAN THIS AGAIN
------------------------------------------------------------
Trigger: the customer taps "Plan this again" on a past itinerary
card.

The decision point is the travel date stored on that itinerary.

7.1 Travel dates are still in the future

  - Do not ask the customer anything.
  - Show a short "Fetching updated prices" state.
  - Create a new deal and a new version, copying the itinerary, the
    route, the nights and the travel dates from the past itinerary.
  - Land the customer on the existing itinerary detail screen, not a
    new screen, with fresh prices in the cost breakdown and the usual
    Save Itinerary action.
  - Flight prices are not re-fetched. Keep the flight component as it
    is and show the existing note that flight prices may be outdated.
  - Hotels and activities are re-priced for the stored dates.

7.2 Travel dates are in the past

  - Show a screen asking for the new travel start date before
    anything is priced, because there is nothing to price until the
    customer says when.
  - Explain why: "This plan was for {date}, which has gone by. Tell
    us when you want to travel and we will price it for those dates."
  - Field: new start date only. Nights are carried over from the
    original plan, with a note that the length can be changed on the
    next screen.
  - The CTA is "Fetch updated prices". It stays disabled until a date
    is chosen.
  - After the date is entered, the flow is identical to 7.1: fetch
    prices, then land on the itinerary detail screen with Save
    Itinerary.

7.3 Common rules for both paths

  - The traveller count is carried over from the past itinerary.
  - Day options and hotel selections are reset. The customer picks
    again against current availability.
  - The new plan is a NEW deal with a NEW Plan ID. The past plan is
    never edited, never overwritten and never removed from Past
    plans.
  - The past deal stays in the lost state. Planning again does not
    revive it.
  - If the customer abandons before saving, no live deal is created
    and nothing changes in My Plans.

7.4 Open decision: one active deal at a time

The system allows only one active deal per customer. If the customer
already has a live deal and taps "Plan this again", we need a
decision from sales and ops. The options are:

  - Allow the new plan to be created and let the consultant merge or
    close the older one.
  - Block it and tell the customer they already have a plan in
    progress, with a link to it.
  - Allow it but flag it for the consultant on the internal tool.

This needs to be settled before release. The prototype currently
creates the new plan without any check.

8. ITINERARY BUILDER AND INTERNAL TOOL HANDLING
------------------------------------------------------------
This is the part that makes the customer facing feature usable by
the sales team.

8.1 Lost status must be visible against the deal

  - When a consultant opens or searches a deal in the itinerary
    builder, the lost state must be clearly marked against the deal
    ID, at the top of the record, not buried in a status column.
  - The marking must be visually distinct, so a consultant never
    quotes from a lost deal by mistake.
  - Every version inside a lost deal inherits the lost marking.

8.2 Search

  - Searching by the customer's mobile number must return lost deals
    as well, not only the active one, with the lost ones clearly
    labelled.
  - Searching by the Plan ID the customer reads out must land the
    consultant directly on that exact itinerary version, not on the
    deal in general.
  - The internal tool must accept the Plan ID in the same search box
    as the deal ID. The customer will not know which of the two they
    are reading out.

8.3 Acting on a lost deal

  - A consultant must be able to see the itinerary and the PDF of a
    lost deal, in read only form.
  - A consultant must not be able to edit or re-quote a lost deal in
    place. To quote again, a new deal must be created, the same way
    the customer's "Plan this again" creates one.
  - When a new deal is created from a past one, the internal record
    must store the source Plan ID, so the consultant can see where
    it came from and the reporting can measure revival.

8.4 Status changes

  - Marking a deal lost in the internal tool must make it appear in
    the customer's Past plans within the normal sync window.
  - Un-marking a deal (moving it back to active) must remove it from
    Past plans and return it to the live list.
  - Only one active deal per customer stays enforced. Past plans do
    not count against that limit.

9. EVENTS TO BE TRACKED
------------------------------------------------------------
Property naming to follow the existing analytics convention. Every
event below carries customer_id, plan_id, enquiry_id and deal_id
wherever they apply.

9.1 Visibility and browsing

  - past_plans_section_viewed
    Fires when the Past plans header enters the viewport.
    Properties: past_enquiry_count, live_plan_count.

  - past_plans_expanded
    Fires when the customer opens the Past plans section.
    Properties: past_enquiry_count.

  - past_plans_collapsed
    Fires when the customer closes the section.

  - past_enquiry_expanded
    Fires when one past enquiry row is opened.
    Properties: enquiry_id, plan_id, country_count, itinerary_count,
    months_since_quote.

  - past_enquiry_collapsed
    Properties: enquiry_id, plan_id.

  - past_itinerary_card_viewed
    Fires when an itinerary card inside a past enquiry is seen.
    Properties: plan_id, destination, version_number,
    months_since_quote.

9.2 Plan ID

  - plan_id_copied
    Fires on tap to copy.
    Properties: plan_id, level (enquiry or itinerary), destination.

9.3 Plan this again

  - plan_again_tapped
    Fires on tap of "Plan this again".
    Properties: plan_id, destination, version_number,
    original_travel_date, dates_in_past (true or false),
    months_since_quote, has_live_deal (true or false).

  - plan_again_date_screen_viewed
    Fires only on the past dates path.
    Properties: plan_id, original_travel_date.

  - plan_again_date_selected
    Properties: plan_id, new_travel_date, days_from_today.

  - plan_again_date_abandoned
    Fires if the customer closes the date screen without continuing.
    Properties: plan_id.

  - plan_again_pricing_started
    Properties: plan_id, path (future_dates or past_dates),
    travel_date.

  - plan_again_pricing_completed
    Properties: plan_id, new_plan_id, new_deal_id, duration_ms,
    old_price, new_price, price_delta.

  - plan_again_pricing_failed
    Properties: plan_id, error_reason.

  - plan_again_itinerary_opened
    Fires when the customer lands on the itinerary detail screen.
    Properties: plan_id, new_plan_id, destination.

  - plan_again_saved
    Fires when the customer saves the revived itinerary.
    Properties: plan_id, new_plan_id, new_deal_id, destination,
    travel_date, price.

  - plan_again_abandoned
    Fires if the customer leaves the itinerary detail screen without
    saving, within the same session.
    Properties: plan_id, new_plan_id, last_step.

9.4 Internal tool

  - builder_lost_deal_opened
    Properties: deal_id, plan_id, opened_by, source (mobile_search,
    plan_id_search, direct_link).

  - builder_deal_created_from_past
    Properties: source_plan_id, source_deal_id, new_deal_id,
    created_by.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT WE WANT TO LEARN FROM THE DATA
------------------------------------------------------------
  - How many customers with a lost deal open Past plans at all.
  - How many go from opening Past plans to tapping "Plan this again".
  - The drop between "Plan this again" and a saved itinerary, split
    by whether the dates were in the past or the future, because the
    past dates path has one extra step.
  - How much time passes between the original quote and the revival.
  - How often the Plan ID is copied, which tells us whether customers
    are actually using it on calls.
  - Revenue from revived plans, tracked through the source Plan ID
    stored on the new deal.

    "Hotels and activities for {date}"

MAP VIEW ON CHANGE HOTEL - REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT
Screen: Change hotel (map view)
Addendum to: Change hotel screen requirement document

------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
------------------------------------------------------------
  - The list answers "which of these is better". It cannot answer
    "where would I actually be", which is the question behind every
    "2.4 km from city centre" on a card.
  - Location decides more than price on a short stay. A cheaper hotel
    40 minutes from everything is not cheaper.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. ENTRY AND EXIT
------------------------------------------------------------
  - A "Map view" button sits in the header of the Change hotel screen,
    with a map icon and the words, not an icon on its own.
  - The map opens over the list, full screen.
  - Back, the List button, and the device back gesture all return to
    the list at the same scroll position. Nothing is lost.
  - The map shows only the hotels currently passing the filters. The
    count in the map header matches the count in the list header.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE PINS
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Every hotel is a pin at its real location.
  - The pin carries the PRICE DIFFERENCE from the stay the customer
    already has, not the price. It is the only money this screen deals
    in.
  - Green with a down arrow when the hotel is cheaper.
  - Red with an up arrow when it is dearer.
  - Grey and the word "Same" when there is no difference.
  - If the stay has no hotel yet, pins carry the total price for the
    stay instead.
  - One line at the bottom explains the colours until a pin is tapped.
  - Pins must not cover each other at the opening zoom. Where two
    hotels sit too close, the map must separate them or cluster them.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. TAPPING A PIN
------------------------------------------------------------
  - The tapped pin fills in, grows slightly, and rises above the rest.
  - A small card slides up from the bottom: photo, hotel name, star
    category, Booking.com score, area and distance from city centre,
    and the price difference. It is the list card, smaller.
  - Tapping the card opens the hotel detail screen, exactly as tapping
    a full card in the list does.
  - Tapping the same pin again, or the map background, closes the card.
  - Only one card is open at a time.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. MAP BEHAVIOUR
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Opens framed on all the hotels shown, never so tight that a pin is
    cut off at the edge.
  - Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, both free.
  - Zooming or panning does not close an open card.
  - A hotel keeps the same spot when filters change. Pins must not move
    around as the list is filtered.
  - Offline or tiles unavailable: show the list with a short message,
    not an empty grey screen.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. EVENTS TO BE TRACKED
------------------------------------------------------------
Every event carries customer_id, deal_id, version_id, itinerary_id,
stay_index, city and current_hotel_id where they apply.

  - change_hotel_map_opened
    Properties: hotel_count, active_filter_count, sort_key.

  - change_hotel_map_pin_tapped
    Properties: hotel_id, price_delta, direction (cheaper, dearer,
    same), distance_from_centre, star_category, booking_score.

  - change_hotel_map_card_opened
    Properties: hotel_id, price_delta.

  - change_hotel_map_card_tapped
    Fires when the customer opens the hotel from the map card.
    Properties: hotel_id, price_delta, pins_tapped_before.

  - change_hotel_map_zoomed
    Properties: zoom_level, direction (in or out).

  - change_hotel_map_panned
    Fires once per gesture, not per frame.

  - change_hotel_map_closed
    Properties: duration_ms, pins_tapped, cards_opened,
    exited_to (list or hotel_detail).

  - change_hotel_map_failed
    Fires when tiles or locations cannot load.
    Properties: reason.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT WE WANT TO LEARN
------------------------------------------------------------
  - How many customers open the map at all, and whether they come back
    to the list or leave from the map.
  - Whether a hotel chosen from the map is nearer the centre than one
    chosen from the list.
  - Whether the map raises the rate of actually changing the hotel, or
    only the time spent on the screen.
  - How often customers move to a dearer hotel after seeing where it
    is.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. OPEN POINTS
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Map provider and key. The prototype uses OpenStreetMap tiles, which
    is fine for a prototype and not for production.
  - Hotel coordinates. The prototype places hotels on a ring at their
    stated distance from the centre because it has no real latitude and
    longitude. Live data must carry both.
  - Whether the city centre, the airport, or the itinerary's activities
    should also appear on the map as reference points.
